Tools for everyday financial decisions.

FinZave provides mathematical calculators to project the numbers before you commit to them.

SIP Calculator

WHAT IT DOES: Projects compounding returns based on regular monthly contributions and estimated annual interest rates.

WHY IT IS USEFUL: Helps visualize the long-term impact of consistent savings over time.

EMI Calculator

WHAT IT DOES: Computes monthly loan repayment amounts based on the principal, tenure, and interest rate.

WHY IT IS USEFUL: Allows you to mathematically determine if a loan installment fits into your current savings capacity.
