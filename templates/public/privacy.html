Privacy Policy

How information is used within FinZave.

Last Updated: September 2026

01 — Information We Collect

We collect information that you explicitly provide when you register for an account and interact with the FinZave application. This includes your account credentials (such as username and email) and the financial records you choose to input.

02 — How Information Is Used

The information collected is used to operate and maintain the FinZave service. This includes managing your account, authenticating your access, and providing you with the core functionality of the application such as calculating metrics and displaying your dashboard.

03 — Financial Information

Your financial information (incomes, expenses, and goals) is used strictly to provide FinZave's tracking, calculations, analysis, and rule-based insights. It is processed to generate the visual reports and feedback presented in your personal dashboard.

04 — Rule-Based Analysis

Your data is processed through predefined, transparent financial rules to evaluate your financial condition. FinZave does not utilize machine learning or train artificial intelligence models on your personal financial data.

05 — Data Storage

Your information is stored in our database architecture to ensure you can access your historical financial records. We employ standard database security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access.

06 — Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial information to third parties. Information may only be disclosed if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of FinZave, its users, or the public.

07 — Third-Party Services

Our service may contain links to or integrate with third-party services. These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices regarding your data.

08 — Data Retention

We retain your personal and financial information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with the services. You may choose to delete specific records or your entire account at your discretion.

09 — User Controls

You have full control over the financial data you input. Through the application interface, you can add, modify, or delete your income and expense records at any time.

10 — Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or service offerings. Any updates will be posted on this page, and your continued use of FinZave indicates your acceptance of the changes.

11 — Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding how your information is handled, please reach out via the feedback channels provided within the FinZave application.
