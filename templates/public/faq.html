FAQ

Everything you need to know about FinZave.

General

What is FinZave?
FinZave is a personal finance assistant that helps you record your income and expenses, and uses rule-based logic to analyze your spending behavior.
Who is FinZave for?
It is designed for individuals who want to move beyond simple expense tracking to actually understanding their financial patterns and saving capacity.
What does FinZave help with?
FinZave helps you record financial data, track goals, analyze monthly spending, and receive transparent insights to support better financial decisions.

Working

How does FinZave work?
You input your income and expenses. FinZave records the data, performs calculations, evaluates your financial state using predefined rules, and presents the insights on your dashboard.
How is savings calculated?
Savings are calculated mathematically by subtracting your total recorded expenses from your total recorded income over a given period.
How does financial analysis work?
Financial analysis aggregates your data to compute metrics like your savings rate and breaks down your spending by category.
How are insights generated?
Insights are generated when your calculated financial metrics match specific, predefined conditions in our rule engine (e.g., if expenses exceed a certain percentage of income).
Does FinZave use machine learning?
FinZave uses predefined, rule-based financial logic rather than a trained machine-learning model to generate insights.

Features

How does expense management work?
You log your everyday expenses with an amount and category. FinZave stores them to calculate your total monthly outflow and categorical spending.
How do financial goals work?
You define a target amount and date. FinZave tracks your current saved amount against the target, providing progress percentages and required monthly saving figures.
What is financial analysis?
It is the process where your raw income and expense records are evaluated over time to show you where your money is going and how well you are saving.
What are SIP and EMI calculators?
These are mathematical tools. The SIP calculator projects compounding returns over time, while the EMI calculator computes monthly loan repayment amounts.
Does FinZave provide investment-related functionality?
FinZave provides mathematical calculators (like SIP) for estimating returns, but it does not execute trades, manage portfolios, or provide investment advice.

Data

What information does FinZave use?
FinZave uses only the financial records you explicitly input, such as your income entries, expense logs, and financial goals.
Can financial records be edited or deleted?
Yes, you retain full control over your data. Any logged income or expense can be updated or removed from your records at any time.
